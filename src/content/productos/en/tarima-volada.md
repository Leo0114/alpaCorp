On wing pallets, support stringers are inset toward the center, leaving deckboard ends overhanging. This load distribution improves weight sharing and increases racking capacity.

## Key Features

- Inset stringers for optimized weight distribution across deckboards.
- Higher load capacity without increasing total lumber volume.
- Compatible with selective racking systems and high-bay storage.
- Available with heat treatment (HT) for export.

## Applications

High-density rack storage, concentrated heavy loads, metalworking, and automotive industries.
