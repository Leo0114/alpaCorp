This hybrid packaging combines heavy-duty wooden bases or reinforcements with high-strength corrugated cardboard walls, striking an optimal balance between weight, cost, and payload protection.

## Key Features

- Wooden base engineered to support heavy payloads and facilitate forklift handling.
- Corrugated cardboard walls that reduce total package weight and shipping costs.
- Easy to assemble, dismantle, and recycle.
- Superior shock absorption and surface protection for packaged goods.

## Applications

Distribution of home appliances, automotive components, electronics, and goods requiring lightweight yet durable containment.
