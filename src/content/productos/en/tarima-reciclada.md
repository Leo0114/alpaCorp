We recover, grade, and recondition pallets to return them to active service in safe operational condition. It represents our most economical and eco-friendly packaging alternative.

## Key Features

- Individual piece-by-piece inspection and structural repair.
- Graded by quality based on wood condition.
- Significant cost savings compared to brand-new pallets.
- Lower carbon footprint by extending lumber life cycles.

## Applications

Internal plant logistics, inter-warehouse transfers, and domestic shipments where new wood is not required.
