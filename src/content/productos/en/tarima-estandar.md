The standard wooden pallet is the backbone of industrial supply chains. We manufacture them using domestic and imported pine wood, selected for dimensional stability and load-bearing strength.

## Key Features

- Standard industry footprints as well as custom dimensions tailored to your plant.
- 2-way or 4-way entry for forklifts and hydraulic pallet jacks.
- Calibrated stringers and deckboards designed for heavy static and dynamic loads.
- Available with heat treatment (HT) certification for international export shipments.

## Applications

Selective rack warehousing, floor stacking, ground and maritime transport of finished goods and raw materials.
