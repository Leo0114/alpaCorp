Block pallets utilize solid wooden blocks as support posts rather than continuous stringers, creating a compact, highly rigid structure that maximizes container floor space.

## Key Features

- Solid block posts enabling seamless 4-way entry for forklifts and pallet jacks.
- Ultra-robust frame engineered for high-density loads.
- Optimal utilization of sea container dimensions.
- Custom manufacturing based on specific payload weight and footprint.

## Applications

Containerized export freight, heavy machinery, automotive components, and high-density products.
