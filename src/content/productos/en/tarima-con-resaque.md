Precision stringer notches allow forklift tines to enter from all four sides, significantly speeding up handling times within warehouses and loading docks.

## Key Features

- 4-way entry accessibility.
- Enhanced speed during stacking and unstacking operations.
- Reinforced structure at critical load-bearing points.
- Tailor-made dimensions to optimize shipping container and rack capacity.

## Applications

High-turnover distribution centers, cross-docking logistics, and active assembly lines.
