Wooden industrial packaging provides exceptional structural protection for goods during transport and warehousing, tailored to exact weight and dimension requirements.

## Key Features

- Impact and vibration resistant structural design.
- Custom-engineered based on total payload weight and center of gravity.
- Facilitates efficient loading, unloading, and warehouse maneuvers.
- Treated and certified wood compliant with international export standards (HT).

## Applications

Protection of heavy machinery, industrial electronic equipment, and long-distance overseas shipments.
