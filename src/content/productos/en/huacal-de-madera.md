Wooden slatted crates protect products on all four sides while enabling easy handling thanks to integrated side handles. It is the classic solution for consolidating loose or irregularly shaped components.

## Key Features

- Manufactured to the exact dimensions of the packaged item.
- Ergonomic side handles for manual handling.
- Optional fixed or removable wooden lid.
- Available with heat treatment (HT) certification for export.

## Applications

Consolidation of loose parts, spare components, agricultural goods, and freight requiring lateral protection.
