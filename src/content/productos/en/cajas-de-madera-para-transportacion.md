Wooden shipping crates provide full containment and protection against external factors, ensuring your products arrive intact at their destination.

## Key Features

- Total enclosure preventing product exposure to environmental conditions.
- Internal structural reinforcements for enhanced stability.
- Optional heat treatment (HT) for international export compliance.
- Customizable designs tailored for forklift and crane handling.

## Applications

Maritime, ground, and air export, medical equipment transport, industrial machinery, and delicate instrumentation.
