We assign dedicated ALPA personnel inside your facility to handle pallet management, scrap collection, and onsite repairs, relieving your internal team of operational burdens.

## What's Included

- Trained staff stationed directly inside your production plant.
- Collection, sorting, and management of wood scrap materials.
- Onsite inspection and immediate repair of damaged pallets.
- Direct coordination with your plant logistics and material handling supervisors.
