Empaques Industriales ALPA operates dedicated in-house heat treatment chambers (HT) required for export-grade wooden packaging. We certify products under NOM-144-SEMARNAT-2017 (ISPM-15), ensuring pest-free compliance for customs clearance.

## What's Included

- In-house industrial heat treatment chamber.
- Official certification compliant with NOM-144-SEMARNAT-2017 (ISPM-15).
- Stamping and official marking of treated packaging.
- Service available for third-party wooden packaging as well.
